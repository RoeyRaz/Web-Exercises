Singer of the Year, Female Singer of the Year, and Band or Group of the Year are musical titles awarded to outstanding artists as part of the annual Israeli Hebrew music charts, usually based on public voting. In the past, these titles often caused intense rivalries between fans. For example, in the early 1980s, there were rivalries between fans of the bands Tislam and Benzin, and later between fans of Ofra Haza and Yardena Arazi. Today, these titles are still considered prestigious and are often used as part of the winners' promotional campaigns. The titles were first awarded in the 1969 annual music chart to singers Avi Toledano and Rivka Zohar, as well as the Nahal Entertainment Troupe. Beginning in 2013, the annual Galgalatz music chart discontinued the categories of Singer of the Year, Female Singer of the Year, Group of the Year, and Breakthrough Artist of the Year. Instead, Galgalatz listeners selected the People of the Year in Music, and a Breakthrough of the Year category was later added. The broadcasts of Kol Israel and Reshet Gimel, which had previously awarded these titles, ended on May 14, 2017, following the establishment of the Israeli Public Broadcasting Corporation. The station that replaced Reshet Gimel, Kan Gimel, continued awarding these titles in its annual music chart. Beginning in 2023, the corporation changed the titles to Selected Artists of the Year and, on one occasion, presented the award to four selected artists.

Female Singers of the Year

Odeya

Odeya Azulay was born and raised in Bat Yam in a religious family and attended a religious school. Around the age of eleven, she became less religious and transferred to a secular school. A few years later, she began reconnecting with religion. In an interview with the Israeli youth website Frogi, she explained that although there are religious restrictions concerning women singing, she has a rabbi who supports and encourages her. She later added that since becoming more religious, she has been writing about deeper subjects, including Judaism, the soul, and God. In an interview with Maariv LaNoar, she said that she had tried to audition for the television program School of Music. In October 2019, she enlisted in the Israel Defense Forces.

Jasmin Moallem

Jasmin Moallem was born in Jerusalem to a musician father. At the age of two, she moved with her family to Portugal, but two years later they returned to Israel and settled in Jaffa. During her teenage years, she attended the Thelma Yellin High School of the Arts, where she studied visual arts. She has a brother and a sister. In 2015, she began studying music in the United States. She also studied at a music academy in London and later attended the Rimon School of Music in Israel. In 2018, she participated in the Shirimon music competition together with Uri Brinker. They performed Omer Adam's song "Noetzet Mabat" and won first place. Moallem is married to Ron Ben Yair, and they have two daughters.

Noa Kirel

Noa Kirel was born and raised in Ra'anana, Israel. Her father, Amir Kirel, owns a glass importing and marketing business, and her mother, Ilana, owns a fashion boutique. She has two older brothers. She was originally named Noya, but when she was two months old, she was diagnosed with a kidney condition. Following the advice of a rabbi, her parents changed her name to Noa. At the age of ten, she auditioned for the Israeli reality television program School of Music, broadcast on Channel 2, but was not accepted. At the age of thirteen, she appeared alongside actress Eliana Tidhar in an advertising campaign for the Israeli cosmetics company Careline.

Eden Ben Zaken

Eden Pessia Ben Zaken, born on June 8, 1994, is an Israeli singer-songwriter, composer, and actress. She became famous after participating in the first season of the Israeli television program The X Factor Israel, where she finished in second place. Ben Zaken also appeared as a guest judge during the show's third season.

Charli XCX

Charli XCX is a British singer and songwriter known for her energetic electronic pop sound. Her music often combines pop songwriting with experimental production, and her albums include Pop 2, how i'm feeling now, Crash and Brat.

Grimes

Grimes is a Canadian singer, songwriter and music producer whose work blends electronic music, synth-pop and experimental sounds. She is known for projects such as Visions and Art Angels and for producing much of her own music.

FKA twigs

FKA twigs is an English singer, songwriter, dancer and performer. Her work combines alternative R&B, electronic music and striking visual performance. Her releases include LP1, Magdalene and Caprisongs.

Male Singers of the Year

Omer Adam

Omer Adam was born in North Carolina, United States, to Yaniv and Sharon Adam. At the age of three, he returned to Israel with his family. He lived in Moshav Mishmar HaShiva until 2017. In 2005, he participated in the singing competition Shir Nolad, performing the song "Yeled Pele" alongside television presenter Tal Mosseri. His father's family is of Caucasian Jewish origin, while his mother's family is of Ashkenazi origin. His father, Major Yaniv Adam, served as deputy commander of the Shaldag Unit and as deputy battalion commander of the 202nd Paratroopers Battalion. His maternal grandmother is the artist Aviva Shemer, and his grandfather is Shmuel Adam, who served as a colonel and senior police officer. Shmuel Adam is a cousin of Major General Yekutiel Adam. Another relative, Yekutiel Rabaev, was killed while defending Petah Tikva in 1917. Omer's brothers, Roee and Gal Adam, are also singers. Omer served in the Israel Defense Forces at Training Base 20 in the Ordnance Corps.

Osher Cohen

Osher Cohen was born and raised in Kiryat Bialik, Israel, to former singer Ilan Cohen. He grew up in a traditional, music-loving family. At the age of five, he began studying at the Kiryat Bialik Conservatory. During his musical education, he learned to play several instruments, including the piano, baglama, guitar, clarinet, and violin. At the age of eleven, he began performing as an accompanying keyboard player at live concerts. In 2017, Cohen enlisted in the Military Police Corps and served as a supply clerk at Military Prison 6. Following a nine- month period of absence without leave, he served a six-month sentence in military prison.

Static

Liraz Russo, born on December 8, 1990, is an Israeli pop singer and rapper best known by his stage name Static, sometimes also called Statboy. He was a member of the musical duo Static and Ben El, together with singer Ben El Tavori. Working with music producer Jordi, the duo released several successful songs that became major hits in Israel. Their music videos received tens of millions of views on YouTube. Some of their most popular songs include "Tudo Bom", "Kvish HaHof", and "Barbie".

Eden Hason

Eden Hason was born on August 25, 1994, in Pardes Hanna-Karkur, Israel. He is an Israeli singer- songwriter, lyricist, composer, and music producer. His musical styles include pop, Mizrahi music, and electronic music. He is a tenor and plays both guitar and piano. Hason began his professional musical career in 2015 and has become a well-known performer in the Israeli music industry. In 2018, he won an ACUM Award for Song of the Year for his debut single "Shemishehu Ya'atzor Oti", which means "Someone Stop Me". In 2022, he performed alongside the singer Mor at the Sultan's Pool concert venue in Jerusalem.
